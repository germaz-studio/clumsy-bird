Clumsy Bird is an open-source arcade game, originally created with MelonJS, now ported to Android for F-Droid by Oskar Gerlicz-Kowalczuk (Germaz Studio).

Tap anywhere on the screen to flap your wings, navigate through pipes, and compete for the highest score.

Features:
* Fullscreen edge-to-edge gameplay with responsive scaling
* Simple and responsive one-touch controls
* On-screen touch sound mute toggle
* Local high score tracking
* 100% Free and Open Source (GNU GPL v3.0)
* Completely offline, zero telemetry, zero permissions required
